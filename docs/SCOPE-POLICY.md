# Scope policy

Holdout Labs, and this toolkit, provide **statistical analysis only**.

- **What an audit does:** it measures how fragile a strategy's historical evidence is (overfitting, multiple testing, holdout behaviour, stability, cost sensitivity). The user chooses the strategy and the declared objective.
- **What it never does:** give trading or investment advice. Reports never say whether to buy, sell, hold or trade any security, future or crypto-asset. They never recommend position sizes or allocations and never make performance claims or promises.
- **Fix lists** describe *what to test* to reduce statistical fragility (for example "evaluate on an untouched holdout"). They never describe what to trade.
- **Disclaimers** appear in every report and on every page of the website.
